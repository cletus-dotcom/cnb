Mr. Joseph Achacoso Rañola

Mr. Joseph Achacoso Rañola, widely known as “JR” to colleagues and peers, is a seasoned public finance expert and consultant with over three decades of extensive experience in the Philippine government’s budget system and public administration.

Mr. Rañola holds a Master’s degree in Public Administration, equipping him with a deep understanding of the structural strengths and systemic challenges of the Philippine bureaucracy. Over the years, he has established himself as a thought leader in budget research, fiscal policy formulation, and legislative engagement. As a lecturer and resource person, he has contributed to national discourse through training programs, policy briefings, and technical consultations with a wide array of stakeholders.

His professional expertise spans the following key areas:

  • National budget research, analysis, and policy development
  • Budget legislation and oversight
  • Local government budgeting, procurement, and accountability mechanisms
  • Strategic planning and executive management
  • Editorial and publication management
  • Graphic design and visual communication
  • Enterprise development and business incubation
  • Leadership in marketing strategy and stakeholder engagement
  • Mentoring, capacity-building, and institutional strengthening

A native of Jagna, Bohol, Mr. Rañola earned his Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (major in Management) from the Lyceum of the Philippines University in Intramuros, Manila. He built his early career in both public and private institutions, serving in various managerial and executive roles that refined his multidisciplinary approach to governance and development.

In 2002, Mr. Rañola was appointed as a Consultant on the National Budget to members of the Supreme Court. This marked the beginning of a long and distinguished advisory career, during which he provided budgetary and policy guidance to senators, members of the House of Representatives, executive agencies, constitutional commissions, and local government officials, as well as domestic and international investors.

Recognizing the need for specialized consultancy in public finance, he founded JAR Consultancy in 2003, serving as President and Chief Executive Officer. His work through JAR Consultancy further deepened his insights into the technical, procedural, and political dimensions of national and local budgeting processes.

Motivated by a strong sense of public service and a commitment to good governance, Mr. Rañola formalized his long-standing advocacy for transparency, equity, and accountability in public budgeting by establishing the Center for National Budget (CNB) in 2006. Originally named the Center for National Budget Legislation (CNBL), the institution has evolved to become a respected resource center in the field of fiscal governance. He also founded CNBL Publications, where he serves as President and Publisher, overseeing the production of budget education materials used by stakeholders across the country.

From 2019 to 2025, Mr. Rañola served as Mayor of Jagna, Bohol, bringing his national expertise in budgeting and governance to the local level. His tenure reflected his core principles of fiscal discipline, participatory governance, and results-driven leadership.

With a career built on integrity, innovation, and public accountability, Mr. Rañola continues to lead the CNB in its mission to democratize knowledge of the national budget and empower Filipinos to become active participants in the country’s development.
