Formerly CENTER for NATIONAL BUDGET

Welcome to the home of Citizens for Nationwide Budget Reform (CNB), a SEC-registered, non-profit, and nonpartisan organization that has championed transparency, equitability, and accountability in the Philippine budget system for nearly three decades.

At CNB, we believe that budget awareness is the cornerstone of good governance. While the national budget is central to our country's development, a persistent gap remains in public understanding. We aim to fill this gap by serving as a dedicated platform for budget literacy and citizen engagement, ensuring that public funds truly serve the people—especially those in underserved communities.
