WHO WE ARE

Three decades at the forefront
of fiscal governance.

The Citizens for Nationwide Budget Reform (CNB) is a non-stock, non-profit, nonpartisan organization registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission. Since our founding, CNB has stood for one core belief: every Filipino deserves to understand where their public money goes—and how to access it.

We engage the budget process through research, policy analysis, stakeholder consultations, and education. We don't just decode the General Appropriations Act. We give LGUs, legislators, and citizens the tools to participate meaningfully in it.

The national budget grows larger each year—yet public understanding of it remains shallow. CNB exists to close that gap.

LEADERSHIP

Joseph Achacoso Rañola

FOUNDER & PRESIDENT, CNB  |  FORMER MAYOR, JAGNA, BOHOL

Joseph "JR" Rañola is one of the Philippines' foremost authorities on the national budget system—with over 30 years spanning public finance research, legislative consultation, and local governance.

In 2002, he was appointed Budget Consultant to the Supreme Court. He has since advised senators, representatives, executive agencies, and LGU officials. In 2006, he founded CNB—formalizing a career-long advocacy for fiscal transparency, equity, and accountability.

From 2019 to 2025, JR served as Mayor of Jagna, Bohol—bringing his national budget expertise directly into local governance. He governed with fiscal discipline, participatory leadership, and results-driven accountability.

WHAT DO WE DO

From our centers of excellence.

CNB works through specialized centers of excellence.

As a Research Center
CNB conducts research on budget history, expenditure program, revenue, appropriations and obligations, borrowing and outstanding debt of the national government.
As a Publishing Center
We publish practical references—primers, briefs, and reviews—so budget information is readable and actionable.
As a Content Provider
CNB conducts in-depth and historical research on the budgets of departments and agencies as well as the budget dimensions of sectoral issues and concerns.
As an Education Center
To inspire and empower every Filipino with the knowledge of the national budget through education.
As a Project Management Center
End-to-end support to help institutions access funding, deliver projects, and strengthen implementation and accountability.
